# Demo Retail Dataset

The included data is synthetic and generated only for this demonstration.

- transaction_id: synthetic unique transaction ID
- date: transaction date
- city: city label
- category: product category
- product: product label
- quantity: units sold
- revenue: gross transaction revenue
- discount: discount amount
- cost: estimated cost
- profit: revenue minus discount and estimated cost
- salesman: synthetic salesperson identifier

Do not describe this synthetic dataset as real customer or business data.
